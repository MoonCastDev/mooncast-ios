PyroWave's native Metal decoder, vendored for MoonCast for iPad and iPhone.

Upstream:  https://github.com/Themaister/pyrowave (Hans-Kristian Arntzen), MIT (LICENSE in this folder)
Commit:    21f099d ("Enable 1.3 in interop tests."), the same pin as MoonCast Android and Moonlight X
Taken:     metal/ (the "AI assisted port of Pyrowave to Metal", which upstream says is minimally maintained
           and may be removed), so it is kept here rather than fetched:
             metal/pyrowave_metal.h, pyrowave_common.hpp, pyrowave_common.mm, pyrowave_decoder.mm,
             pyrowave_bitstream.hpp, pyrowave_bitstream.cpp, shaders/pyrowave_msl.h
           unchanged. The encoder (pyrowave_encoder.mm) and the .metal sources are left out: the decoder compiles
           the MSL in shaders/pyrowave_msl.h at run time (generated by upstream's metal/shaders/transpile.sh).

MoonCast's own files here (GPL-3.0, like the rest of the app):
  MCPyroWave.h / MCPyroWave.mm   the device probe, the per-frame decode and the YUV to RGB presenter on a
                                 CAMetalLayer, and the GPU boost. The record framing parser in it is ported
                                 from MoonCast Android's pyrowave_bitstream.h (from Moonlight X, GPL-3.0).
